Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 June 2026

SEO Health (“SEO Health”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a sole proprietorship registered in India (Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-KL-05-0042131), based in Kanhangad, Kasaragod, Kerala. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email us at hello@seohealth.in.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password when you create an account.
  • Business information you provide — the business name, location, website, and the listings or profiles you ask us to analyze.
  • Audit and usage data — the results of the visibility audits we run for you, your preferences, and basic logs needed to operate and improve the service.
  • Publicly available competitor data — when you add a competitor, we retrieve publicly available business information (such as ratings, review counts, website details, and public social-profile metrics) to provide comparison insights.

2. How we use third-party platform data (Google and Meta)

To produce your audits and insights, we use data from third-party platforms. We access only publicly available information and only what is needed to provide the service.

  • Google — we use Google APIs (including PageSpeed Insights and Places) to assess your website's performance and your public business-listing information.
  • Meta / Instagram — we use the Instagram Graph API (the business_discovery endpoint) to retrieve publicly available metrics about business and creator Instagram accounts, such as follower counts and post counts, for competitive insight. We access only public data, we do not collect private user information, and we never post or take actions on your behalf.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the service and run the audits you request.
  • To generate insights and send you results and notifications you have opted into.
  • To respond to your support requests and communicate with you about your account.
  • To keep the service secure and to comply with our legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the product — for example, hosting, email delivery, and the API providers listed above — and only as needed to provide the service. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing hello@seohealth.in.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and choices

You can access, correct, export, or delete your information, and withdraw consent where applicable, by emailing hello@seohealth.in. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and a minimal amount of analytics to understand how the service is used. We do not use cookies to sell your data.

9. Children

The service is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “last updated” date above and post the new version on this page.
